DROP YOUR OWN SOUNDS HERE
=========================

Any audio file in this folder (.wav, .ogg or .mp3) whose name matches one below
replaces the sound the game generates in code. Leave out any you don't have.

Use only sounds you are allowed to ship (CC0 from Pixabay / Freesound, or your own recordings).

  screech   - the jump-scare hit (loud, short, shrieking)
  scream    - a human scream
  boom      - deep impact under the jump scares
  slam      - door slamming
  thud      - body hit by the train
  breath    - slow breathing down the phone (about 7 s)
  whisper   - a whisper right behind you
  hum       - a child humming
  static    - phone-line static
  ring      - old telephone bell (loops)
  horn      - train horn
  rumble    - train running (loops)
  wind      - night wind (loops)
  crickets  - crickets (loops)
  drone     - low horror drone (loops)
  heart     - one heartbeat (loops)
  rope      - rope creaking under weight (loops)
  creak     - door creaking open
  knock     - knock on wood
  step      - one footstep
  sting     - musical horror sting
  clank     - lever / points clank
  murmur    - muffled voice on the phone (loops)
